## 🏆 The Problem

Governments receive enormous amounts of citizen feedback through voice, text, surveys, grievance systems, local channels, and other fragmented sources.

The problem is not simply collecting this information.

The real problem is turning it into **decision-grade infrastructure intelligence**.

A citizen may report:

> “Our village hospital is too far away.”

Another citizen reports the same problem in a different language. Hundreds or thousands of people may be experiencing the same underlying infrastructure gap.

Traditional systems tend to treat these as individual complaints.

**JANVISTA treats them as signals.**

It combines citizen demand with geographic, demographic, infrastructure, and investment context to answer a much more important question:

> **Where are the most significant unmet infrastructure needs, why are they occurring, and what should policymakers investigate or prioritize next?**

# 🧮 Priority Intelligence Engine

The **JANVISTA Priority Engine** is the core decision-intelligence layer.

It combines measurable signals into a transparent priority score.

An illustrative scoring configuration is:

| Signal | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Citizen Demand | 30% |
| Infrastructure Gap | 25% |
| Population Vulnerability | 15% |
| Accessibility | 15% |
| Urgency | 10% |
| Investment Mismatch | 5% |

The weights are configurable and auditable.

The important architectural principle is:

> **The analytical engine calculates the score. AI explains the score.**

JANVISTA does **not** ask an LLM to arbitrarily decide which district deserves public investment.

Instead:

```text
Raw Data
   ↓
Features
   ↓
Analytical Model / Priority Engine
   ↓
Priority Score
   ↓
Evidence
   ↓
Gemini Explanation
```

This improves reproducibility, transparency, auditability, and trust.

# ⚖️ Bias & Representation

Citizen demand is not automatically equivalent to social need.

A digitally connected population may generate more requests than an underserved population with limited digital access.

JANVISTA therefore considers signals such as:

- population normalization
- data coverage
- accessibility
- vulnerability
- geographic representation
- under-reporting risk

The platform surfaces uncertainty instead of hiding it.

Example:

> **Data confidence: Moderate**
>
> Some regions may have lower reporting coverage or incomplete infrastructure data.
